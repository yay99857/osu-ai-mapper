# Sliders, spinners and new combos

## Sliders

- **Slowdowns**: lowering slider velocity for sustained notes or held vocals (a long note becomes
  a slow, long slider rather than a fast one or repeats).
- **Kick sliders** (short sliders, sometimes repeating, on regular beats) for rhythmic punch.
- **Pattern techniques**: half-beat sliders separated by rests, parallel sliders, slider trains,
  segment sliders chained end to start, zig-zagging sliders.
- **Shapes**: S-curves/waves, spirals, windmills, cutting or crumpled paths — mostly visual
  variety; keep the path readable and the reverse arrows visible.
- Slider ends should land on something in the music.

## Spinners

- Spinners resolve a passage: before a break, at the end of the song, over a held or rising
  sound. A **pre-spinner note** a fraction of a beat before the spinner can carry the beat while the
  spinner resolves it; **post-spinner notes** follow long spinners near the end.
- Double spinners occur before breaks or at the end. Leave recovery time after a spinner (ranking
  minimums: 4/2/1 beats for Easy/Normal/Hard).

## New combos

- Usually one combo per musical phrase (think one line of lyrics), most often starting on a
  downbeat (the long white tick in 4/4); typical combos hold 2-10 objects, not dozens.
- A new combo also signals a change, like the start of a jump pattern or a spacing change.
- Combo colours: 2-8, readable on the background; the colour change itself is part of reading.
