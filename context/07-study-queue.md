# Study queue

Material found but not read or watched yet, and what to look for in each.

## pishifat, osu!mapping video series (126 episodes, 2016-2019)

The most cited mapping course. Index: [osu!mapping](https://osu.ppy.sh/wiki/en/Community/Video_series/osu!mapping),
videos on [pishifat's channel](https://www.youtube.com/user/Pishifat). No transcripts were read;
do not claim their content until watched or transcribed. Most relevant to us, in order:

1. Concepts; Spacing Emphasis; Basic Spacing; Basic Rhythm; Irregular Rhythm — the foundations.
2. Flow + Circles; Flow + Sliders; Jumps; Streams; Variety; Overmapping; Filler Rhythm.
3. New Combos; Progression; Spread; Difficulty Spikes; HP Drain; Circle Size; Slider Velocity;
   Slidershapes; Playfield Usage.
4. Genre episodes: "Wub" Maps, Hip Hop, Drum N Bass, Breakcore, Swing Songs, Anime, Metal;
   Accelerating BPM; RLC & Following Music; Reasons Behind Music; Hitsounding & Rhythm Choice.
5. Mapper talks: Sotarks & Not Only PP (relevant to our Sotarks model), Mismagius & Difficult Maps.
